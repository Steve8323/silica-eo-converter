Silica-only electro-optic → photonic bandwidth converter

Platform: fused-silica waveguides (n=1.444) in air + electrodes. Nothing else. All FDTD in MEEP 1.29 (2D, Ez/TE), local. Electrodes enter as refractive-index perturbations Δn on the silica (DC Kerr surrogate); the honest Δn↔voltage map is in §5. Addition only by evanescent coupling — the layout contains zero Y-junctions.

Headline results
• Zero-GVD core width w* = 0.991 µm → shortest 5 mm-safe pulse 16.4 fs FWHM = 26.8 THz optical bandwidth (exact β(ω) propagation, β₃-limited).
• Master MRR is buildable in silica: bend-limited Q reaches 1.1×10⁷ at R=16 µm (FDTD Harminv), far above the 4×10⁵ needed to resolve a 100 V/µm Kerr shift.
• Coupler-calibrated MZI sweeps its signed output across [-1, +1] with ~2% excess loss; footprint 92×14 µm in-sim.
• χ₃ four-wave mixing in the ring generates the first comb sidebands on the neighboring resonances (boosted-χ₃ FDTD; real-silica threshold scaling in §6).

2 — Directional coupler (the only combiner)

Exact supermode (transfer-matrix) design + FDTD calibration. Straight-section rate 0.0767 rad/µm at gap 0.30 µm (supermode predicted 0.0724); the raised-cosine s-bend approaches contribute a fixed 0.375 rad per coupler, so the 50/50 straight length is LC = 5.35 µm (FDTD-calibrated κ(L) linear fit, power conserved to 0.1%). Smaller gap → shorter coupler → flatter over the 26.8 THz band: the size and bandwidth optima coincide.

5 — Electrode reality check (the honest part)

Silica is centrosymmetric: no Pockels. With electrodes the EO effect is the DC Kerr effect Δn = 3χ₃E²/(2n), χ₃ = 2.5×10⁻²² m²/V². A DC bias Eb linearizes it (effective Pockels ∝ Eb).

Consequences. (i) The fast EO port of this chip is the ring, not the MZI: at Q=10⁶–10⁷ a 50–100 V/µm swing switches a resonance by a full linewidth. (ii) MZI Δn values simulated here (10⁻²) are electrode surrogates; at real Kerr strength the same transfer curve needs cm-scale arms or is used as a slow (bias-set) weight bank — set once, then static for inference. (iii) Photon lifetime trades against sensitivity: f3dB = ν/2Q ≈ 0.10 GHz at Q=10⁶. The converter therefore takes a ≤GHz electrical drive and spreads it over 27 THz of optical comb bandwidth — a ∼10⁴× bandwidth conversion.

6 — Master-MRR χ₃ comb seed (FDTD)

Two CW pumps on adjacent R=8 resonances (1548.6 / 1517.3 nm); silica χ₃ (boosted for FDTD visibility) generates the degenerate-FWM sidebands 2f₁-f₂ (1581.3 nm — independently confirmed as the ring's next resonance by Harminv) and 2f₂-f₁, and then cascades into a full comb: FSR-spaced lines across >55 THz (fig). Real-silica scaling: parametric threshold Pth ∝ n²V/(n₂λQ²); silica microtoroids (Q∼10⁹) osculate at mW pump — the literature Kerr-comb regime this master MRR targets.

Methods & caveats

  • All FDTD: MEEP 1.29, 2D (slab), Ez/TE, PML 1.5 µm, res 16–30 px/µm as noted.
  • Dispersion: exact slab solve; FDTD-validated (fig §1). 5 mm propagation is spectral-domain with the exact β(ω) — a 5 mm FDTD cell is not tractable and not needed for linear propagation.
  • Electrode Δn values in ring/MZI sims are surrogates (labeled); the physical map is §5. No field solver for the electrode gap was run — E is taken as V/gap with overlap Γ=0.8.
  • χ₃ comb demo uses boosted χ₃ (labeled) — FDTD cannot reach Q=10⁹ timescales.
  • 2D: no substrate leakage or sidewall roughness; fabricated silica Q values quoted from wedge/toroid literature for the scaling arguments only.
